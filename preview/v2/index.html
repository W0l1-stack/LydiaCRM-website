        FAQ

        Questions, answered.

        Do I need to be good with tech?

        No. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use Lydia. Add your contacts, connect your email or SMS, and switch on a ready-made follow-up.

        What happens after the free trial?

        You get 14 days with everything switched on. After that, choose Starter or Pro to keep going. No card is needed to start.

        Can I use my own email and SMS provider?

        Yes. Lydia works with Resend, SendGrid and Mailgun for email and Twilio, Vonage and others for SMS. Bring your own accounts, or start with ours.

        How does the AI work?

        Lydia's AI drafts replies, sorts incoming messages and suggests next steps based on your real customer history. By default it waits for your approval; it only acts on its own where you have given it permission, and you can take that back at any time.

        Is my customer data safe?

        Every business on Lydia is fully separated from every other, and your provider keys are encrypted at rest.

        Can my team use it too?

        Yes. Starter includes 3 team members and Pro has no limit. Assign conversations, deals and visits so everyone knows who is handling what.
