The story every scene follows

  1. Oluchi Afolabi asks Bloom & Co. about bridal glam for her wedding on Saturday 19 December, and whether she can pay in two parts.
  2. Amara sends quote Q-0001 ($1,280). Oluchi approves it on WhatsApp with a 50% deposit.
  3. The quote becomes job J-0001 at her home in Lekki: trial tomorrow, the wedding morning with Kemi and Tobi, then four monthly facials.
  4. Deposit invoice INV-0001 ($640) is issued, her transfer is recorded and verified.
  5. An automation tags her and creates the "book the trial" task, and its run shows each step.
  6. She opens a help desk ticket to move the trial to Saturday morning.
