Common questions

Can I manage one-time and recurring work?

Yes. Jobs distinguish one-time and recurring work. Use visits to schedule and assign the work that happens on a particular date.

What can customers see in their portal?

With eligible access, customers can review service information such as quotes, upcoming visits and invoices linked to them.

Can my team review what an automation did?

The Execution Inspector shows the recorded evidence for a workflow run. Select a step to review available inputs, outputs and decisions.
